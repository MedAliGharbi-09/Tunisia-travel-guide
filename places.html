Hammamet

Beach

A relaxed resort town with a walled old medina right next to the sea.

Nabeul

Beach

Known for its pottery and Friday market, with a long beach close by.

Djerba

Beach

A large island in the south with calm water, olive groves and traditional villages.

Carthage

History

The ruins of the ancient city that once rivalled Rome, just outside Tunis.

El Djem

History

A huge Roman amphitheatre that is one of the best preserved in the world.

Sidi Bou Said

History

A hilltop village with blue and white houses and views over the bay.

Douz

Desert

The gateway to the Sahara, with camel rides and dune views.

Tozeur

Desert

A palm oasis town with brick architecture, near salt lakes and film locations.
