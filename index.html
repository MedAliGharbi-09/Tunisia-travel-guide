Welcome

Tunisia sits on the Mediterranean coast of North Africa. In one trip you can swim at white sand beaches, walk through ancient Roman ruins and ride a camel into the Sahara. This guide covers the best places, the food and how to plan your trip.

Highlights

Beaches

Hammamet, Nabeul and Djerba have warm water and long sandy beaches.

Food

Try brik, couscous and lablabi, plus the famous Tunisian spicy harissa.
