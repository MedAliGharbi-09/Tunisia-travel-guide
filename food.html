Food to Try

Brik

A thin fried pastry filled with egg, tuna and parsley. Eat it hot.

Couscous

Steamed semolina served with vegetables and lamb, fish or chicken.

Lablabi

A chickpea soup with bread, cumin, olive oil and harissa. A popular breakfast.

Ojja

Eggs cooked in a spicy tomato and pepper sauce, often with merguez sausage.

Culture

Nabeul pottery

Nabeul is the pottery capital of Tunisia. Workshops sell hand painted plates, bowls and tiles.

Jasmine

Jasmine is the national flower. In Hammamet, sellers offer small bunches of jasmine to visitors.

Souks

Covered markets sell spices, leather, carpets and gifts. Bargaining is normal.
